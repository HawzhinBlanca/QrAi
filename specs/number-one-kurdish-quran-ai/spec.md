This specification consolidates the active readiness, Flutter/Node consolidation, DR, and migration
programs. Approval authorizes planning only; implementation follows `plan.md` one task at a time.

## Objective

Deliver one scholar-governed Flutter + Node Quran-learning product that is genuinely native in
Kurdish, keeps sacred content deterministic, exposes only calibrated/reviewed AI findings, works on
real devices and weak networks, and earns launch/#1 claims through independent evidence.

## Non-negotiable scope

- Flutter is the final Android/iOS/Web client; Node is the final API/realtime/worker boundary.
- Canonical Quran text is immutable, byte-exact, versioned, checksummed, and never generated by an LLM.
- Sorani and Kurmanji receive full core parity; Badini is separately scoped and never implied by “Kurdish.”
- Automatic recitation judgments remain disabled below the approved evidence/calibration gate.
- Humans—not agents—approve scholarship, law/privacy, security closure, accessibility, SRE evidence,
  research ethics, learner labels, pilot release, and the #1 verdict.

## Proposed 10/10 evidence thresholds

These are QrAi acceptance gates, not claims that a cited standard mandates each number:

- Quran quote/reference integrity: 100%; invented or misattributed Quran/Hadith: zero.
- Held-out recitation: lexical precision ≥99.5%, recall ≥98%, correct-recitation false correction
  ≤0.5%; Tajweed/phoneme macro-F1 ≥95%, every supported rule/slice ≥90%, ECE ≤0.03,
  largest approved subgroup gap ≤3 percentage points, p95 feedback ≤1 second.
- Operations after launch: rolling 180-day learning-session success and crash-free sessions ≥99.95%,
  sync ≥99.99%, user-progress RPO ≤5 minutes/RTO ≤30 minutes, Quran corpus RPO=0.
- Learning: two adequately powered independent studies, measurable 30/90-day retention, lower 95%
  confidence bound above zero versus the best comparable app, plus non-inferiority to the approved
  qualified-teacher pathway.

## Completion rule

“10/10” is a gate state, not an average. A failure in sacred-content integrity, theological safety,
child/privacy/security, or candidate evidence blocks release regardless of strengths elsewhere.
“#1” additionally requires independently reproduced head-to-head superiority on the same protocol.
