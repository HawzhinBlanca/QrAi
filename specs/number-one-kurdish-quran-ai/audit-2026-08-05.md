# Brutal audit: path to the #1 Kurdish Quran-learning AI

**Audit date:** 2026-08-05<br>
**Scope:** web/mobile learner experience, Quran integrity, Kurdish readiness, ASR/alignment/tajweed truth, pedagogy, teacher/scholar review, privacy/security, operations, evaluation, competitors, and 2026 research frontier.<br>
**Method:** Serena symbol/reference mapping, repository/data/ledger inspection, canonical verification attempts, and current primary research/product sources. No product code or tests were changed.

## Executive verdict

This is **not currently a top-three Quran-learning product and not currently a Kurdish-first product**. It is a promising, safety-conscious platform prototype with much better governance engineering than most AI demos.

The hard rating is **3.5/10 overall today**. The underlying Quran/safety platform is roughly **7/10**, but production reliability proof is **4.5/10**, Kurdish readiness is **1/10**, acoustic tajweed feedback is **2/10**, and real-world validation is **1/10**.

The largest truth gap is simple: the learner flow calls `predictTajweed` without learner audio or recognized speech. The endpoint scans canonical Quran text and reports where rules are expected. It does **not** establish whether the learner performed those rules correctly. A polished UI cannot turn canonical-text rules into acoustic evidence.

There is still a credible path to #1 because no independently benchmarked Kurdish-first Quran AI leader was found. The moat is not “more AI.” It is the first consented, expert-adjudicated Kurdish learner corpus plus calibrated selective prediction, excellent Kurdish pedagogy, teacher review, scholar sourcing, and public proof.

## What is genuinely strong

1. **Canonical Quran discipline.** The manifest contains 114 surahs, 6,236 ayahs, and 82,456 stored tokens. The app preserves canonical bytes, excludes non-recited marks from scoring without deleting them from display, and has dedicated normalization/mark invariants.
2. **A real alignment core.** `alignWords` performs bounded global sequence alignment, handles missed/extra words, avoids silent mushaf marks, and does not fabricate a perfect recitation when recognition is absent.
3. **Safety gates with the right shape.** `canShowLearnerFacingAiOutput` uses an approval allowlist and requires confidence plus sources. Unknown review states fail closed.
4. **Privacy engineering.** Recording consent, guardian/external-ASR gates, raw-audio retention classes, tenant-scoped audit, export/delete, and no-secret/no-raw-audio logging tests are substantial.
5. **Tenant isolation.** Postgres RLS is forced, the application role cannot bypass RLS, and proxy code overwrites client tenant/consent claims from trusted actor/session state.
6. **Real teacher components.** Sessions, audio, alignments, findings, notes, accept/reject/edit, and audited status promotion exist.
7. **Engineering honesty in the current ledger.** The readiness ledger says no candidate exists, does not convert automated text into human sign-off, and keeps 34 of 51 release items open.

## The critical product truth gaps

### 1. Tajweed is annotated, not heard

`AuthenticatedApp/runAlignmentAndTajweed` sends recognized words to alignment, then calls tajweed with only the session and Quran range. `predictTajweed` runs `analyzeAyah` on canonical words. Its fixed confidence constants are rule heuristics, not calibrated probabilities. Persistence explicitly records an `analysis_basis` of canonical text.

What the system can honestly say now: “This word contains an expected madd/ghunnah/qalqalah/etc. rule.”

What it cannot honestly say now: “You pronounced this rule correctly/incorrectly.”

The immediate product correction is to label these as **rule guidance**, never **learner mistakes**, until acoustic evidence and evaluation clear the review gate.

### 2. Kurdish-first is not shipped

- `apps/web/src/locales/ckb.json` is empty and the locale capability manifest hides Kurdish from ordinary selection.
- The real Sorani translation bundle covers 856 of 6,236 ayahs (13.7%), across 39 files, with one recorded omission.
- Flutter advertises English only.
- No Sorani/Badini explanation curriculum, culturally localized onboarding, teacher language workflow, child/guardian copy, or Kurdish-L1 pronunciation benchmark exists.

CLDR 48 distinguishes Central Kurdish/Sorani and Kurmanji across scripts/regions; an undifferentiated “Kurdish” toggle is not enough. Iraqi Kurmanji/Badini also needs Arabic-script handling. [CLDR 48 release notes](https://cldr.unicode.org/downloads/cldr-48)

### 3. The pedagogy is mostly a state machine

The UI names listen, guided recite, memory recite, correction, drill, and completion, but several stages render nearly the same content. The current passage is always the first up-to-seven ayahs, learners can jump stages, memory mode still exposes the text, drill lacks a verify/repeat/mastery loop, and the memorization plan contains semi-static values. Mutashabihat receives an empty verse set.

There is no placement assessment, Arabic-letter/makhraj progression, child/adult differentiation, minimal-pair practice, error-specific micro-lesson, hifz plan builder, teacher assignment, or comprehension check.

### 4. Realtime transport is not realtime intelligence

The gateway provides real chunk transport, acknowledgements, retry/backpressure, and retention. The learner does not receive live partial recognition or per-word alignment; the web path records, stops, uploads/transcribes, then analyzes. The “live” internal card summarizes capture with an empty alignment list.

### 5. Teacher and scholar loops are incomplete

- Teacher findings are associated to the selected attempt by repeated canonical `wordId`, which can mix attempts.
- `transcriptSource` and `analysisBasis` exist in backend data but are omitted from the teacher summary/UI.
- Alignment persistence is fire-and-forget while tajweed follows immediately, creating a race.
- Web learners do not reload later approved findings; Flutter reloads only immediately after stopping.
- Scholar approval APIs exist, but the scholar screen is a placeholder without audio/evidence/model/rule/version review and retraction/expiry workflow.

## Reliability audit: why “10/10” is not supportable

The reliability rating is **4.5/10**, despite strong defensive code.

1. **Fresh deployment schema drift:** Compose initializes through migration `0021`; CI applies `0022`–`0025`; the platform has no runtime migration runner.
2. **False-green readiness:** ASR model-load failures leave `/health` at HTTP 200 with `loaded:false`, while Compose checks only the status code. ML health is similarly shallow.
3. **Unbounded ML→ASR call:** `transcribeAudio` uses `fetch` without a timeout/abort. The outer proxy can time out while the inner work continues.
4. **Replay protection degrades open:** gateway Redis dedup falls back to per-process memory; shipped Compose defines no Redis and fail-closed is off.
5. **Single-host, non-atomic evidence:** audio plus metadata and JSONL audit writes are filesystem-based; audit failure does not fail the request. This is not HA or tamper-evident.
6. **Monitoring is a demo:** Prometheus scrapes only the platform API, alerts are API-centric, and no Alertmanager/receiver/on-call proof exists.
7. **No release proof:** no candidate, release authority, candidate-bound load/chaos/DR evidence, or independent challenger run exists.
8. **Supply-chain gaps:** no Python audit gate, no blocking container scan, non-blocking/floating SBOM generation, and the experimental neural stack has recorded advisories that block promotion.
9. **Gate omissions:** the canonical script omits the golden regression and some Python audio guards; DB and Flutter coverage are environment-dependent.

The canonical gate was run during this audit. The sandboxed attempt was inconclusive because package-manager verification and socket binds were blocked, so it was rerun unrestricted. **`bash scripts/verify.sh` exited 0 with `VERIFY OK`**: Rust fmt/clippy, all TypeScript typechecks, contracts 31/31, Quran data 43/43, web 191/191, Node services, Rust, gateway/audio, live-Postgres integration and parity suites, production build, bundle-secret/header guards, and all 527 JS/Rust dependency-license checks passed. This is strong implementation evidence; it is still not a candidate-bound release, real learner evaluation, SLO/DR proof, or human approval.

## What 2026 research actually says

There is no credible “10/10 accurate” automatic tajweed system today.

- The public [IQRA 2026 challenge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29087) reports a best F1 of about 0.72, with false acceptance around 0.30. Authentic learner-error data and careful labels beat fashionable audio-LLM hype.
- [IqraEval 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.arabicnlp-sharedtasks.61/) used about 2.2 hours from 18 native Arabic speakers deliberately producing errors; its best F1 was below 0.48. It contains no Kurdish-L1 or child validation.
- A promising [Quran pronunciation preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.00094) claims 850+ hours and a Quran Phonetic Script, but automated data creation is not a substitute for independent learner validation.
- A controlled [forced-alignment comparison](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19363) found Montreal Forced Aligner outperforming WhisperX and MMS. “Newest model” is not automatically the most reliable timestamp source.
- [Meta Omnilingual ASR](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/omnilingual-asr-open-source-multilingual-speech-recognition-for-1600-languages/) is useful for Kurdish teacher speech, transcription, and data bootstrapping. It is not a Quran recitation judge.
- [sherpa-onnx](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/index.html) already supports Flutter/iOS/Android streaming/offline deployment and is the most direct route to private on-device verse tracking.
- NIST calls for representative human-subject evaluation, documented TEVV, production monitoring, human oversight, recalibration, and removal—not a single headline accuracy number. [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)

The key invention lesson is to build **two independent engines**:

1. A Quran-constrained streaming ASR/position engine for verse tracking, omissions, repetitions, and memorization.
2. A phone-level mispronunciation/tajweed engine for acoustic assessment, trained and evaluated on authentic learner errors.

Low WER does not prove makhraj, vowel, ghunnah, qalqalah, tafkhim, or madd correctness. Never convert the first engine's text match into the second engine's pronunciation judgment.

## Competitive reality

- [Tarteel](https://tarteel.ai/) leads real-time memorization tracking and claims missed/incorrect/skipped-word detection. Its own support center says the AI is **not trained to recognize tajweed mistakes**. [Tarteel tajweed limitation](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414457-tarteel-is-not-recognising-my-tajweed-mistakes)
- Tarteel also states that audio is cloud-stored, a portion may receive human review, and AI-training opt-out is Premium-only. Privacy-by-default can be a real differentiator. [Tarteel AI-training opt-out](https://support.tarteel.ai/en/articles/12414504-how-to-opt-out-of-ai-training)
- [AL Siraat](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.alquran.aiquran.quranlearning) has 1M+ downloads and markets realtime word/tajweed feedback, but no common public learner benchmark or calibration report was found.
- [Qara'a](https://apps.apple.com/iq/app/qaraa-1-learn-quran/id1520521429) combines curriculum, AI checking, and real ustadz review—the blended teaching experience this app must match or beat.
- [Al Quran](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greentech.quran) has 10M+ downloads, 60+ languages, extensive translation/tafsir, and sets the content/completeness bar.

No independently benchmarked Kurdish-first recitation AI leader was found. That is genuine white space, not proof this product already owns it.

## The architecture that can truly win

### A. Kurdish teaching OS, not a translated dashboard

- Ship full native-reviewed Sorani (`ckb`) first, then Badini/Kurmanji for Iraq (`ku-Arab-IQ`), with explicit dialect/script selection.
- Form a paid Kurdish curriculum council: qari/teacher, Sorani educator, Badini educator, child-learning specialist, accessibility reviewer, and scholar.
- Keep canonical Quran Arabic immutable. Localize navigation, explanations, exercises, privacy, teacher notes, and pedagogy—not the Quran bytes.
- Build a placement path from letters/makhraj → short vowels/sukun/shaddah → words → ayahs → tajweed → hifz/revision.
- Use native contrast audio and Kurdish explanations for mistakes that Kurdish-L1 learners actually make.

### B. A unique, consented Kurdish learner evidence moat

Create a versioned corpus with:

- Sorani and Badini/Kurmanji learners; children and adults; beginner through advanced.
- Real phones, microphones, rooms, noise, network conditions, and speech/sensory accessibility cases.
- Arabic-native comparison cohorts without conflating accent with error.
- Double independent annotation, measured inter-rater agreement, and expert adjudication.
- Speaker-, ayah-, device-, and site-disjoint held-out sets; no synthetic-error leakage.
- Explicit licensing, revocable training consent, retention/deletion, and child/guardian governance.

Synthetic errors may augment training. They must never be the gold test truth.

### C. Selective prediction instead of universal claims

Every acoustic result must be one of:

- **High-confidence correct**
- **High-confidence targeted correction**
- **Uncertain — ask a teacher**

Calibrate per rule, population, device, and noise slice. Optimize false acceptance because telling a learner an error is correct is the most harmful failure. Abstaining is a feature.

### D. Evidence-native teacher and scholar review

Each finding should bind:

- exact session/attempt and audio segment;
- canonical byte range and qira'ah/version;
- recognized phones and alternatives;
- calibrated confidence and uncertainty reason;
- model/dataset/checksum;
- analysis basis (acoustic vs canonical guidance);
- teacher decision, scholar scope, expiry, and retraction history.

Teachers need contrast playback, targeted drill assignment, learner history, and unresolved queues. Scholars need rule/model/source approval tied to exact versions—not a generic topic record.

### E. Private-by-default inference

- On-device streaming verse position when the target phone passes latency/battery/thermal tests.
- Ephemeral cloud audio by default when server assessment is required.
- Separate, revocable training opt-in; never sell privacy as a premium feature.
- Child profiles default to minimal collection and guardian controls.
- Preserve the existing export/delete and tenant/RLS strengths.

### F. A grounded Kurdish tutor

Use generation only over scholar-approved Kurdish lesson units and approved Quran/tafsir sources. Require byte-exact Quran quotation validation, citations, review state, and abstention when evidence is absent. IslamicEval shows that Quran/Hadith hallucination and correction are separate hard problems; generic RAG is not enough. [IslamicEval 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.arabicnlp-sharedtasks.67/)

## A defensible “10/10 reliability” standard

Ten-out-of-ten must mean **all release evidence is satisfied**, not magical zero-error AI. Proposed release targets—not current results—should include:

| Dimension | Proposed release bar |
|---|---|
| Learner-visible AI provenance | 100% source/model/data/review binding |
| Unsafe or unsourced learner output | 0 |
| Cross-tenant leakage | 0 in adversarial and production monitoring |
| Acoustic false acceptance | Predeclared per-rule/population ceiling; selective abstention used to meet it |
| Teacher agreement | Predeclared and independently measured on representative held-out data |
| Calibration | Reliability diagrams/ECE plus child/dialect/device/noise slices |
| Availability | Approved SLO/error budget with measured candidate load/chaos results |
| Data lifecycle | 100% tested consent, retention, export, deletion, retry, and audit paths |
| Recovery | Scheduled encrypted off-host backups plus timed restore meeting approved RTO/RPO |
| Accessibility | Automated + keyboard + VoiceOver/Safari + alternative reader + RTL/reflow audits |
| Release proof | Signed candidate evidence, clean-checkout challenge, rollback rehearsal, named owners |

Do not choose numeric acoustic thresholds after seeing the test set. The scholar/product/safety team must predeclare them, and the app must withhold output where the frontier cannot meet them.

## Strategic sequence to #1

### First: make every claim literally true

- Rename current tajweed output to canonical rule guidance.
- Surface acoustic/canonical analysis basis everywhere.
- Fix session-bound finding identity, persistence ordering, and later review delivery.
- Unify deployment migrations; make ASR/ML readiness deep; bound/cancel ML→ASR calls.

### Second: become genuinely Kurdish-first

- Complete native-reviewed Sorani UI/curriculum and RTL/accessibility.
- Add Badini/Kurmanji Iraq support as a distinct locale/product path.
- Recruit teacher/scholar councils and begin the governed learner corpus.

### Third: ship the dual-engine MVP

- On-device or edge streaming Quran position engine.
- Server-side phone-level assessment for only the first validated rule subset.
- Calibrated abstention, teacher escalation, targeted contrast drills, and learner inbox.

### Fourth: publish proof and earn trust

- Versioned public benchmark protocol and model cards.
- Per-rule precision/recall/false-accept, calibration, dialect/age/device/noise slices.
- Independent qari/teacher replication and adversarial challenge.
- Bounded Erbil/Duhok pilot with stop rules, daily review, privacy/support monitoring, and rollback.

### Fifth: scale the product moat

- Adaptive hifz planner from real review history.
- Teacher cohorts, assignments, progress evidence, and family/child controls.
- Expand rules only after each rule clears the same evidence bar.
- Expand qira'at only with separate canonical data, models, scholar scope, and evaluation.

## What not to do

- Do not call canonical rule detection “AI tajweed correction.”
- Do not treat low WER or nearest-ayah retrieval as pronunciation accuracy.
- Do not hand-author impressive evaluation numbers or use synthetic errors as the held-out truth.
- Do not bolt on a generic audio LLM and let it issue religious judgments.
- Do not promise all tajweed rules, all Kurdish dialects, or all qira'at before versioned evidence exists.
- Do not hide uncertainty; make “ask a teacher” a first-class outcome.
- Do not spend the next cycle on visual polish while Kurdish content, acoustic truth, and release reliability remain below 5/10.

## Final positioning

The winning claim is not “AI replaces the Quran teacher.” It is:

> **The most trustworthy Kurdish Quran-learning system: private by default, Quran-constrained, acoustically honest, teacher-backed, scholar-sourced, and explicit when uncertain.**

That position is achievable. The current app has unusually good bones for it, but the proof, Kurdish product, pedagogy, and acoustic tajweed engine still have to be built.
