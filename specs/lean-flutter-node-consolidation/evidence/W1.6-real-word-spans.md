## Result

Session transcription now preserves measured ASR word text, confidence, and positive millisecond
spans instead of reducing the response to text. Long PCM sessions are divided into bounded
90-second core windows with two seconds of context; each recognized word is assigned by midpoint
to exactly one core, so overlap duplicates are removed without deleting legitimate repetitions.
Offsets are absolute to the session timeline.

The path fails closed when audio is missing, chunk timing has an interior gap, sample rates are
mixed or unsupported, ASR spans are malformed, recognized text cannot be force-aligned exactly,
or the alignment service is unavailable. Text-only ASR output is force-aligned only against the
recognized transcript itself—never against the expected canonical passage.
