## 2. Byte-reproducible input pair

The source is the tracked CC0 `AlFatihatulKitab.ogg` fixture. The reference is read directly from
the raw word array for canonical 1:1 and joined without trimming or Unicode normalization. The
source manifest also binds a tracked mono 16 kHz PCM derivative by byte length and SHA-256. The
complete vector declaration is `tests/fixtures/audio/muaalem-shadow-vectors.json`; its Node-core
derivation slices that immutable PCM at integer sample boundaries and writes a fixed 44-byte WAV
header, so ffmpeg build metadata and decoder/resampler rounding are outside the fixture contract.

| Vector | Derivation | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---|
| Correct recitation | samples 136,000–239,999; mono 16 kHz PCM S16LE | `a2d9f96034caf5b05f2ab8e2db6eb96a98926b2936354a1d09ff8748cf6fb172` |
| Seeded alteration | zero samples 8,000–19,839 (0.500–1.240 s, half-open) | `1a99050a8313aae8e205b19a5565aee1d9ea348cac889fb0a16ba9fa2867f233` |

Required Ubuntu CI exposed that the original ffmpeg-produced WAV was not portable: the muxer
embedded a version-specific `Lavf` tag and different builds changed a few resampled PCM bytes. The
audit also proved that ffmpeg's timeline-enabled filter actually muted complete internal frames at
0.512–1.280 s, not the declared 0.500–1.240 s. Schema v2 corrects both defects. The exact image was
rerun on the truly declared half-open interval; no old model output was relabelled as new evidence.

Reproduction proof:

canonical-reference digest, binds the active model artifact, and refuses to classify the fixture

## 4. Brutal finding: upstream sifat score corruption

The first exact run correctly refused because 12 of 150 sifat `prob` values were outside `[0,1]`,
with a maximum of `2.0`. Inspection at the pinned upstream commit found the reason in the
length-mismatch branch of `quran_muaalem/decode.py`: after building `new_probs`, it assigns
`ref_aligned_ids[phonemes_mask]` to the probability variable. Class ids therefore escape through a
field documented as probability.

The adapter does not clamp, reinterpret, or call these values confidence. It retains the categorical
sifat label/index and emits `scoreStatus=withheld-upstream-decoder-bug`; all numeric sifat scores are
discarded. Independently produced phoneme softmax values remain finite `[0,1]` raw shadow values.
Promotion is blocked until the decoder is fixed or independently replaced and re-evaluated.

## 7. Protected verifier repair

A closure audit on 2026-08-08 found one real mismatch between the approved W1.10 plan and the
executable gate: the exact image had been built and exercised, but `scripts/verify.sh --release`
did not reproduce that proof. The repair adds `scripts/acoustic-candidate-proof.mjs`, its fail-closed
unit contract, and one release-only invocation. The harness rebuilds the current candidate target,
refuses dirty image inputs, regenerates both sample-exact vectors, and runs one offline non-root
container with no source mount, a read-only root filesystem/input mount, all capabilities dropped,
and `no-new-privileges`.

An isolated pre-commit exercise bound to candidate SHA
`07c69a500193f0be2e5e3d49eafaa344c18c64f4` succeeded against Linux/ARM64 image
`sha256:7ddf7fe2e0aaabac48ed05d1f1e19c785f542a08b36c31a54dd0fb0dd3a29443`. The correct and altered
observations matched the committed structural record exactly, contained no learner finding or
confidence, and withheld all 280 sifat numeric scores. Cold/warm-altered wall observations were
9,089/2,656 ms; six memory samples observed a 560.6 MiB peak/final footprint. These remain
single-host engineering observations only. W1.10 stays unchecked until the repair commit itself
passes the canonical verifier and required exact-SHA CI.
