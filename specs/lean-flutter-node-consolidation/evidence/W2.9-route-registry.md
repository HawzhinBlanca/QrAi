# W2.9 evidence — one executable route registry and Rust-free standalone mode

**Status:** complete; local, image, and exact-candidate remote proof are green<br>
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Delivered boundary

- `server/src/routes/index.mjs::ROUTES` is the sole hand-maintained executable route registry.
  Its method/path projection is the frozen, mechanically derived `ROUTE_KEYS`; the duplicate
  `PORTABLE` allowlist and its source parsers no longer exist.
- With no `PLATFORM_API_UPSTREAM`, the application is genuinely standalone: it registers every
  executable route, keeps unmatched requests local, and cannot select a partial route subset.
- An explicit upstream selects compatibility mode. Only in that mode may `NODE_API_PORTED` choose
  a local subset and only in that mode may unmatched requests or no-database pilot-cookie auth
  delegate to Rust.
- The current executable transition registry has 41 operations: all 38 retained baseline
  operations, learner history, and two manifest-declared agent operations whose retirement gate is
  still blocked. The three owner-gated device operations remain absent; no implementation or
  cutover claim was fabricated.
